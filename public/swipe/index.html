Swipe each word. Don't tap letters.

    How to swipe

    Turn it on

    iPhone
    1. Settings → General → Keyboard
    2. Turn on Slide to Type (it is usually on already)
    Android (Gboard)
    1. Open Gboard settings (hold the comma or globe key, or Settings → System → Keyboard)
    2. Glide typing → turn on Enable glide typing
    Samsung Keyboard
    1. Settings → General management → Samsung Keyboard settings
    2. Swipe, touch, and feedback → Keyboard swipe controls → Swipe to type

    Tips

    • Put your finger on the first letter and slide through the rest. Lift at the last letter.
    • Aim for the shape of the word, not each key. The keyboard figures it out.
    • Don't worry about spaces. The keyboard adds them between swiped words.
    • Double letters: just wiggle or pause on the letter.
    • Wrong word? One backspace deletes the whole swiped word.
    • Go slow first. Speed comes after the shapes are in your thumb.

    Scoring

    • Each correctly swiped word is worth 10 points. Swipe 5 in a row and the multiplier climbs, up to x5.
    • Tapping letters or backspacing resets your combo.
    • ★ finish the level   ★★ mostly swiped and accurate   ★★★ also hit the speed target
    • Speed Rounds and the Daily Challenge go on the arcade High Scores table.
