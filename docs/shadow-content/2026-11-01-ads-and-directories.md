## A. Google Ads, Search campaign

### A1. Setup, step by step

1. In Google Ads, click New campaign. Goal: Website traffic (or "Create a campaign without guidance" if you'd rather skip the wizard).
2. Campaign type: Search.
3. Name: tell_launch (matches the utm_campaign tag on every link below, so it lines up in Google Analytics).
4. Networks: untick Display Network. Untick Search Partners too for the first weeks, so every click comes from Google search and is easy to read.
5. Locations: United States and Canada. Under Location options, choose "Presence: people in or regularly in your targeted locations" rather than "interest in".
6. Language: English.
7. Budget: daily budget of **$[Brew decides] per day**.

### A4. Negative keywords (campaign level)

Single words are broad match negatives (they block any search containing that word). Google does not add plurals to negatives, so a few appear twice on purpose. Paste one per line:

```
movie

printed
canvas
painting
mug

```

Why some of these are here:

- free: the reading is paid. People who want only free tools will click and leave.
- translator, translate, device, collar, button, buttons, board, toy: people looking for talking buttons, collars or "dog translator" gadgets, or expecting a real translation. Not what this is.
- psychic, medium, communicator, telepathy: we make no such claims, and Google restricts that category.
- death, died, dead, passed, loss, grief, memorial, "rainbow bridge", heaven, euthanasia: a grieving person should never land on a joke page from an ad. (The Bridge is not advertised here.)
- vet, sick, symptoms, pain: health searches. This is never veterinary advice.
- book, song, lyrics, movie: "if my dog could talk" is also the name of books and songs.
- print, printed, canvas, painting, mug, shirt: people shopping for physical custom pet products.
- jobs, job, career, hiring, salary: job seekers.

After the first week, open Search terms in Google Ads and add any new mismatches here.
